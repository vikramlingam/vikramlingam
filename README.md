# Hi, I'm Vikram 

I'm a **Data Scientist** with a passion for building custom AI solutions at the intersection of technology and finance. I create my own tools and write about my journey in AI, ML, and finance.

---

### What I Work On

* **AI Agents:** Researching and building autonomous AI agents.
* **LLM Fine-Tuning:** Training and fine-tuning small language models (like Microsoft's Phi-series) for specific tasks in finance, accounting, and tax.
* **Custom AI Tools:** Building open-source, AI-powered applications from scratch, such as custom web browsers and personal search engines - Check perceive search repo.
* **Content Creation:** Writing articles and creating videos about AI, Machine Learning, and FinTech for my Medium and YouTube channels.
* **Financial Automation:** Applying machine learning to automate complex financial processes.
* **AI-Product Prototyping:** Exploring and building new AI-driven business ideas, like my concept for a WhatsApp-based audio news summary service and on-device TTS model that can clone any voice.
